TreeForge

About TreeForge

TreeForge is a free tool that turns a plain-text folder structure into real files. You can download the result as a ZIP or push it to GitHub in one go, and everything runs in your browser.

Who makes it

TreeForge is built and maintained by Aditya, a developer who wanted a fast way to scaffold projects and upload folders from a phone. The source code is public on GitHub.
