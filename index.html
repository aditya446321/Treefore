How it works
  1. Input: paste tree output (├── └── │ or a plain indented list) or upload your folder.
  2. Preview: check the structure. A name ending in /, or with children, is a folder.
  3. ZIP: download the whole structure in one click.
  4. GitHub: connect with a token, pick or create a repo, and everything is pushed in one commit. Empty repos work too.
